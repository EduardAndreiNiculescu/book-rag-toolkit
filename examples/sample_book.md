# A Small Guide to Loyal Customers

CHAPTER 1: WHY PEOPLE COME BACK

Customers return when the second visit feels easier than the first. Habit beats discounts. A coffee shop that remembers your order wins more repeat visits than one that shouts about prices.

Repetition builds trust. Trust lowers the effort of choosing.CHAPTER 2: PRICING WITHOUT DISCOUNTS

Discounts train customers to wait for the next discount. Instead, raise the value of the offer. Add a bonus, shorten the wait, or remove a risk with a clear guarantee.

A guarantee is a promise that moves risk from the buyer to the seller. When the risk moves, conversion goes up.CHAPTER 3: REWARDS THAT WORK

Stamp cards work because progress is visible. People hate leaving a card half full. Start every new card with two free stamps and completion rates rise.

Digital wallet cards keep the progress in the pocket. Nobody forgets a card that lives on the lock screen.
